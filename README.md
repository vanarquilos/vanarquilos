### Build Taste

- Local-first when accounts or cloud do not make the product better.
- Calm interfaces, useful defaults, readable states, and clear visual hierarchy.
- Honest documentation with real product boundaries.
- Small APIs, reviewable changes, and practical QA over unsupported claims.

Also part of OLFU QC Phoenix collegiate League of Legends championship runs, including Campus Rivals 2025 and Estudyante Esports: The National Championships Season 1.

Still learning, especially in deeper repair and engineering work. The point is not to look finished. The point is to keep improving in public.
